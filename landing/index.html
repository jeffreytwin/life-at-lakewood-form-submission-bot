Is the Village Match really free?

Yes. There’s no cost, no obligation and nothing to sign. It’s the easiest way to get real numbers on the villages that fit you before you spend a weekend touring.

Will you flood me with calls and emails?

No. One local agent reaches out with your Village Match and answers your questions. If you’d rather not hear from us, just say so or reply STOP, and we’ll stop.

Is Lakewood Ranch in Manatee County or Sarasota County?

Both. Lakewood Ranch spans the Manatee–Sarasota county line, which runs roughly along University Parkway. The county a home sits in affects its property taxes and its public school district, so it’s one of the first things we check.

What’s the difference between CDD fees and HOA dues?

Many Lakewood Ranch homes have both. HOA dues fund the homeowners association: common areas, amenities and management. A CDD (Community Development District) assessment shows up on your property tax bill and typically pays down the bonds that built the village’s roads, utilities and other infrastructure, plus ongoing upkeep. Both vary by village and even by lot, so we pull the actual numbers for any home you’re serious about.

Do I need my own agent to buy a new-construction home?

You’re not required to have one, but the builder’s on-site sales team represents the builder. Your own agent can help you compare builders and lots, negotiate incentives and upgrades, and line up independent inspections. Many builders require your agent to come with you, or register you, on your first visit, so call us before you tour the models.

Should I buy a resale home or build new?

It depends on your timing and priorities. Building new gets you the latest floor plans and a builder warranty, but it can take many months and comes with a lot of design decisions. A resale home can often close in 30 to 45 days, with mature landscaping and upgrades already in place. We’ll help you compare both side by side.

Which Lakewood Ranch villages are 55+?

Lakewood Ranch’s age-restricted villages include Del Webb, Del Webb Catalina and Cresswind. If you’d rather not live in an age-restricted community, we can show you villages with low-maintenance homes and an active social scene for all ages.

Are you affiliated with the Lakewood Ranch developer?

No. Life at Lakewood is an independent real estate team with Coldwell Banker Realty, and we’re not affiliated with the official developers of Lakewood Ranch. That means our advice about villages and builders is our own.

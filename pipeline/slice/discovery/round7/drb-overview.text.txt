The Only Premier Builder in Seaire Offering Multi-Generational Suites on the Community's Largest Homesites



Welcome to Biscayne Landing at Seaire, a luxury single-family home neighborhood in Parrish, Florida. Designed for contemporary living, Biscayne Landing by DRB Homes offers beautifully crafted homes with seamless access to resort-style amenities. Whether you're looking for a peaceful retreat or an active lifestyle, Biscayne Landing delivers the perfect balance of both.




Just steps from Biscayne Landing is Seaire’s spectacular 4-acre Crystal Lagoon, a stunning destination for paddleboarding, relaxing on sandy shores, or enjoying a sunset stroll. Lagoon living brings a resort-style atmosphere to everyday life, where crystal-clear water and vibrant gathering spaces create a truly unique community experience.




Beyond the lagoon, Seaire offers amenities designed for daily enjoyment, including walking trails, golf cart paths, and planned commercial spaces that will introduce convenient shopping, dining, and services right within the community.




DRB Homes offers a collection of thoughtfully designed homes that blend coastal inspiration with modern functionality. Choose from one- and two-story floorplans featuring open-concept layouts, flexible multigenerational suites or lofts, and covered outdoor living spaces perfect for relaxing or entertaining.




Beyond the floorplan, homeowners can personalize their home with a wide range of designer finishes. Select from beautiful cabinetry, flooring, countertops, fixtures, and color palettes to create a space that reflects your individual taste and style.




Located near I-75, Biscayne Landing provides easy access to Bradenton, Sarasota, St. Petersburg, and Tampa, while beaches, parks, and nature preserves are just a short drive away.




DRB Homes is committed to delivering a seamless homebuying experience from your first visit to move-in day. Experience lagoon living for yourself at Biscayne Landing at Seaire by touring our professionally decorated model homes and exploring the community firsthand.
